SLIDE 08 • CLASSROOM EXPERIMENT

Experiment: Make a Rubber Band Vibrate 🧪

STEP 1

Stretch a rubber band around an empty box or pencil box.

STEP 2

Pluck the stretched rubber band in the middle with your finger.

STEP 3

Observe carefully: You can see it moving rapidly back and forth!

STEP 4

Listen closely: A clear humming sound is produced!

Interactive Pluck Demonstration

Experiment Conclusion: Pluck Band ➔ Creates Vibrations ➔ Sound Is Produced!

SLIDE 09 • CAUSE & EFFECT

What Happens When Vibration Stops? 🛑

STATE 1: VIBRATING OBJECT

Rubber Band Plucked

〰️ Rapid Vibration Active 〰️

SOUND IS HEARD 🔊
STATE 2: OBJECT STOPPED

Hold Rubber Band Still

❌ No Movement / Motion

NO SOUND (SILENCE) 🔇

💡 Key Scientific Rule: "When the vibration stops, the sound ALSO stops immediately!"

SLIDE 11 • HUMAN BIOLOGY

How Do We Make Sound When We Speak? 🗣️

Human Voice Box (Larynx)

Inside our throat are two stretched folds called Vocal Cords.

How Voice Is Created:

When we talk or sing, air from our lungs forces its way through the vocal cords, causing them to vibrate and produce sound!

🖐️ Try This Class Experiment:

Place two fingers gently on your throat and say "AAAAAA" out loud.

Click Here to Test Vocal Vibration Sound 🔊

Main Takeaway: "Our human voice is also produced by vibrating vocal cords!"
